How HoliPlanner works

1 · Colour your days

On the Calendar, click or drag across days (Shift-click for a range, ⌘/Ctrl-click to add). Then press a place on the right to colour them. Add places with + Add location.

2 · Add plans

Select one day and type what you’re doing in the Morning, Midday, Afternoon and Evening boxes. Drag a plan to another slot to move it.

3 · Rearrange

Press Rearrange (or press and hold a coloured day). Everything wiggles: drag a whole place and the others make room. Hotels move with it. Undo is always offered.

4 · Travel

Wherever the place changes, a journey appears on the Travel tab. Pick airports or stations with search (or click the map), set the date, mode and cost. Warnings tell you what’s missing.

5 · Hotels and budget

Drag ⠿ to reorder rows, or use Sort. Hotel and travel costs add up in the Budget with an optional cap.

Spelling doesn’t matter

Type a town or country however it sounds (“Amsterdm”, “Barthelona”, “Pollund”). The app guesses, fixes the spelling, and adds the flag and airport. 🎤 lets you say it. Easy read makes the letters clearer.

Keyboard

Arrow keys move between days · Space toggles · Shift+arrows extend · Esc clears · Alt+arrows move a block in Rearrange mode · ? opens this help.

Everything saves automatically in this browser. Use Export to keep a copy or move to another device.
